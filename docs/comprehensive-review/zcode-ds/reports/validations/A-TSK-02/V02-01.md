## Claim

Every live EKO surface (TUI primary agent, GUI primary agent, pooled agents,
Tauri `update_tasks`, background service) reaches `task_create`/`task_update`/
`task_list` through the same framework `TaskRevisionService` with EKO policy
hooks, and execution semantics (patch engine, PlanValidator, CAS) are not
reimplemented in EKO.
