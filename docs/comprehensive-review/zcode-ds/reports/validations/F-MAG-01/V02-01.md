## Conclusion

The traces are verified and disjoint: handoff and topology are self-contained
public framework APIs with zero production consumers; handoff's dispatch is a
spawn-and-wait around `agent.execute` with no cancel/timeout/events/hooks/result
contract, while `agent_tool` dispatches through the unified subagent lifecycle.
Supports F-MAG-01-P1-01 (detached lifecycle), F-MAG-01-P1-02 (parallel routing
authority), F-MAG-01-P2-01 (context preservation), F-MAG-01-P2-02 (topology
cannot see agent-to-agent calls). Does not prove runtime behavior under load or
concurrency; V03/V04 cover invariants and executable checks.
