## Conclusion

The matrix shows the recovery pipeline is solid at the atomic-write level and
in the task-claim steady state, but breaks at: checkpoint poisoning (row 2),
torn ledger tail (row 5), mid-wave faults (row 6), pause-in-wave (row 7),
store-root consistency across the workspace lifecycle (rows 9-10), and
frontend projection identity (row 12). This supports the task report's answer:
identities survive restart in the steady state, but the recovery paths
themselves contain the P1 defects, and the deletion/retention scope has an
independent P1 gap (V04).
