content is explicit: either a surfaced error with preservation, or a

## Conclusion

Corrupt-file behavior splits cleanly into two families: the hardened
`FileConversationStore` (explicit errors, preservation) and the pre-hardening
`FileStore`/`FileRuntimeStateStore`/ledger (silent empty or hard brick,
with overwrite of the only copy). The single most damaging case for identity
continuity is the `events.jsonl` torn tail, which takes the task run's
identity (and every projection derived from it) permanently offline. This
validation supports the V03 family of the task report.
