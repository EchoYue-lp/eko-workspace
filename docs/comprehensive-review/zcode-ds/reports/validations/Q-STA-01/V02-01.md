## Deviations

The scan targets variable/expression indexes; fixed-literal indexes on
fixed-size arrays (e.g. `octets()[0..3]` in security.rs) were skipped after
spot-verification. serde_json `Value[key]` indexing is null-safe and was not
counted as a risk.

## Conclusion

No **new** unguarded direct-index panic was found in either repository; the
single known production index panic remains the archived F-EXT-03-P1-03
(with additional unreported failure modes n=1/2). Supports the claim; does not
prove absence of panics via slices/arithmetic (V03/V04).
