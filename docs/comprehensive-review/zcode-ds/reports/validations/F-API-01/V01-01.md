## Deviations

1. `tools::shell` and `tools::files` are unconditional `pub mod` re-exports
   of `echo_tools` modules that are `#[cfg(feature = "shell"/"files")]`-gated
   in `echo-tools/src/lib.rs:25-32`. The checks passed only because
   `echo_execution` declares `default = ["files", "shell"]`
   (`echo-execution/Cargo.toml:22`), which activates `echo_tools/shell` and
   `echo_tools/files` through feature unification (`cargo tree -e features`
   confirms `echo_execution feature "shell"/"files"` → `echo_tools`). The
   root features `shell`/`files` (`Cargo.toml:92-93`) therefore gate nothing
   at the facade and cannot disable these modules.
2. The `workspace` facade exposes only 5 of the 7 split crates and inner
   alias-module naming is inconsistent (`core`/`state`/`orchestration`/
   `integration`/`execution` present in 11 facades, absent in
   `scheduler.rs`/`sandbox.rs`/`lsp.rs`).

## Conclusion

The facade is a coherent, single-authority re-export surface: no parallel
implementations and no undocumented duplicate concepts were found (aliases
above are deliberate compat paths). It compiles in the advertised minimal
posture. The two deviations are real contract defects captured as findings
`F-API-01-P2-02` (unconditional shell/files re-exports) and
`F-API-01-P3-03` (incomplete `workspace` facade).
