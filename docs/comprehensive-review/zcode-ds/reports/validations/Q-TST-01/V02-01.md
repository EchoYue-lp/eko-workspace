## Claim

A 15-20 test sample can be classified into meaningful-assertion, restates-
implementation, and weak/zero-assertion categories with file:line evidence.

## Method

Read the assertion bodies of the sampled tests in full (sed ranges around the
test functions) and classify:

- `meaningful`: asserts a production invariant that would fail if the
  production behavior regressed, with a fixture that resembles real input;
- `restates implementation`: asserts behavior derived from (or identical to)
  the implementation's own structure — passes even when the real contract is
  violated;
- `weak/zero-assertion`: no assertions, or assertions on toy scaffolding that
  production never touches.
