## Claim

Workspace-switch and duplicate-promotion fixtures behave deterministically:
switching workspace replaces the projection content without stale residue
(and the fixture exists and passes for instruction and, ideally, hot memory),
and each promotion channel skips facts already promoted by the other channel
(no fact is promoted to both hot MEMORY.md and learned-rules.md).

## Method

Executed test suites plus fixture-coverage inspection:

```text
cd echo-agent-cli && cargo test -p echo-agent-app-core --lib --locked unified_memory   (4 tests)
cd echo-agent-cli && cargo test -p echo-agent-app-core --lib --locked rule_promoter    (3 tests)

hot-memory workspace-switch twin fixture in unified_memory.rs; whether any
Dreaming/promotion test asserts a PROMOTED_TO_RULE-marked warm entry is NOT
promoted to hot.
```

A-MEM-01-P1-01 (V02).
