## Claim

Every arithmetic operation and state transition in the retry/backoff, budget,
and circuit-breaker primitives is overflow-safe and transition-consistent per
AGENTS.md (no unwrap/expect/panic; checked/saturating arithmetic).

## Method

Line-by-line inspection of:
`echo-core/src/retry.rs`, `echo-core/src/budget.rs`,
`echo-core/src/circuit_breaker.rs`, `echo-core/src/utils/{hash,time,json_parse}.rs`,
`src/agent/react/run/retry.rs`, `echo-orchestration/src/tasks/executor.rs:125-141`,
`echo-integration/src/channels/.../feishu/long_poll.rs:137-189,675-682`,
`echo-integration/src/channels/channels/qq/channel.rs:137-188`.

## Expected Result

All arithmetic guarded (checked/saturating/wrapping) or provably bounded by
invariant; no panic path on malformed input.
