## Claim

The instruction/hot-memory projections, the layered memory runtime, and the
Dreaming schedule are registered and reachable on every live EKO surface
(GUI, TUI, REPL, headless/CLI), and every hot-layer mutation trigger fires a
projection refresh that actually updates the affected projection.

## Expected Result

Every surface registers its memory/instruction surface; every trigger that
mutates the hot layer (MEMORY.md) ends with a refresh of the hot-memory
projection on the primary AND pooled agents; the correct projection function
matches the mutated domain (instructions vs hot memory).

## Deviations

The refresh-target mismatch on every hot-layer trigger is the core observed
deviation (P1-01). The channels-mode Dreaming gap is recorded (P3-01).

## Conclusion

Supports: registration and reachability of all surfaces; boot/switch/exit
refresh both projections; ALL in-session hot-layer mutation triggers refresh
the wrong projection (instruction) or nothing, so the hot-memory projection
is stale after every hot-layer change (P1-01). Does not execute any runtime
session; the trace is static but unambiguous.
