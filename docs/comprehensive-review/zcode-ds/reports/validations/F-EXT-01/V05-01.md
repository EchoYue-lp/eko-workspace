## Claim

Tool-level retry has a single authority (the `ToolFailure` classification
consumed by `ToolManager`), and any additional backoff math is either
non-duplicative or explicitly cross-referenced with F-REL-01's unified retry
direction.

## Method

```text
1. Compare F-REL-01 (complete) with the tool-execution retry path found here:
   echo-execution/src/tools.rs:26-46 (retry_delay_ms) and :667-728 / :805-912
   (retry loop gated on ToolFailure::allows_automatic_retry).
2. Map the three authorities from F-REL-01-P2-01 (echo_core::retry for
   providers, src/agent/react/run/retry.rs retry_llm_call for engine LLM,
   echo-orchestration tasks backoff) against the tool-level path.
3. Check whether any other tool-level retry exists in either repository
   (grep for "allows_automatic_retry" and "retry_delay_ms").
```

## Expected Result

Exactly one consumer of tool-failure retry classification; the tool-level
backoff math is either the same `RetryPolicy` or a documented domain-specific
delay that F-REL-01's unification direction should absorb.

## Result

- Exit code: not-applicable (static cross-reference)
- Summary:
  - The tool-level retry **classification** authority is single:
    `ToolFailure::allows_automatic_retry` (`echo-core/src/tools/mod.rs:154-162`)
    is consumed only by `ToolManager` (`echo-execution/src/tools.rs:707,884`).
    No other caller exists in either repository.
  - The tool-level retry **backoff math** is a fourth implementation:
    `retry_delay_ms` (`echo-execution/src/tools.rs:26-46`) — saturating
    arithmetic, exponent cap 5, 30 s cap, `DefaultHasher`-based jitter. It
    duplicates the concept of `echo_core::retry::RetryPolicy` (which
    F-REL-01-P2-01 already found duplicated by `retry_llm_call`). It is
    overflow-safe (unlike the engine copy) but does not reuse `RetryPolicy`,
    so a future policy change must be made in three to four places.
  - F-REL-01's handoff explicitly assigned "tool-level retry semantics keep
    one authority" to this task: the classification authority is confirmed
    single; the remaining duplication is the delay math, which F-REL-01-P2-01's
    direction (migrate onto `with_retry_if`/`RetryPolicy`) should also absorb
    here.
  - No other tool-level retry loop exists (grep for `allows_automatic_retry`
    hits only `echo-core/src/tools/mod.rs` and `echo-execution/src/tools.rs`).
- Log/artifact: none.
