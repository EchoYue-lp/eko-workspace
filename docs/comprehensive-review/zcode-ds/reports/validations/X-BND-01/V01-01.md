## Claim

Across both repositories, each concept family from the X-BND-01 concept list
(task/plan/todo, subagent, approval, memory, skill, plugin, MCP,
context/compression, workflow, intent, notebook, plus worker terminology) has
one authoritative type/trait owner, and every same-name cross-repository pair
is either a deliberate adapter projection or a documented duplicate, never an
undocumented second authority.
