## Claim

There is exactly one approval-arbitration authority per EKO surface family and
no second decision engine; every approval/permission/HITL concept is defined
once and either registered on a live path or provably dead. GUI-configured
permission rules have no consumer beyond their own storage.

## Expected Result

- Approval transports are provided only by app-core `hitl/` (dispatcher,
  repl/tui/channel providers) plus the Tauri command layer; no second decision
  engine exists anywhere.
- `IpcAuth::require_full_auto` / `require_not_strict` have no callers (the
  historical over-gating is gone).
- `permission_rules` storage has no consumer outside the panels commands.
- `PermissionRuleConfig` helper methods have no callers outside their
  definitions.
- No `Bubble` usage in EKO; no `HumanGate` anywhere.

## Deviations

  this is a duplicate transport implementation, not a duplicate decision
  engine — recorded as finding A-HITL-01-P2-01.
- `IpcAuth`'s module doc (error.rs:6-8) claims commands "are gated behind
  `IpcAuth::require_full_auto()`"; the gate API is dead — recorded as
  finding A-HITL-01-P2-04.

## Conclusion

Supports: single decision authority (framework `PermissionService`, consumer
mapping per F-HITL-01 V02) with per-surface transports; no parallel decision
engine in EKO; GUI permission-rule management is definition-plus-storage only
(dead policy); `IpcAuth` gates are dead; EKO never uses Bubble; `HumanGate`
fully removed. Does not prove runtime behavior of the transports (V02/V03
cover reachability and behavior).
