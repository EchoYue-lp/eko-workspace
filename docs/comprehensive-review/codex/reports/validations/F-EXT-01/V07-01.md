## Claim

results through the live ReAct event and model paths.

## Method

short-circuiting, `AgentRunSnapshot::execute_tool_with_policy`, concurrent and
serial `run_tools`, `AgentEvent::{ToolResult,ToolError}`, and
`Message::tool_result`. Compared with an unsuccessful `ToolResult` produced by
an executed tool.

## Expected Result

Argument failures produce `success:false` plus
`ToolFailureCategory::InvalidArguments`; policy blocks use a distinct typed
blocked/cancelled outcome and cannot increment success accounting.

  emits `AgentEvent::ToolResult` and a normal tool message, while no

before execution, precisely where generic validation would need it.
