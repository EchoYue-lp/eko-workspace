## Claim

Resolved Rust duplicate versions are inventoried without equating every
transitive duplicate with a defect.

## Method

Parse each committed `Cargo.lock`, group package names by distinct resolved
version, and list families with more than one version.

## Result

- Exit code: 0
- Framework: 38 duplicate-version families. Largest include `hashbrown` and
  `windows-sys` (five each), `quick-xml` (four), and `getrandom`/`rand` families
  (three).
- EKO: 76 families. Largest include `windows-sys` (six), `hashbrown` and
  `quick-xml` (five), and multiple platform target crates (four).
- Representative meaningful splits include multiple `schemars`, `reqwest`, and
  terminal/parser generations, but attribution is primarily transitive.

## Conclusion

The closures have material version multiplicity. Treat it as a measured budget
and prioritize public-type, native, crypto, parser, and size boundaries; the raw
family count alone is not a finding.
