## Method

`IntentRouter::classify`, TriggerSupervisor, LlmIntentClassifier, LlmClient
helpers, and ChatRequest construction.

Cancellation or a bounded classifier deadline ends pre-routing LLM work and
falls back without delaying entry indefinitely.

- No classifier/router timeout, deadline, or cancellation select exists.

cancellation contract nor a framework-bounded intent deadline.
