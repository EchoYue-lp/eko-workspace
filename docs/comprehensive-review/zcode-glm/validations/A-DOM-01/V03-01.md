## Claim

When arxiv, Semantic Scholar, PubMed, OpenAlex, Crossref, Europe PMC, or
Zotero connectors fail (transport error, non-2xx HTTP, parse error, missing
field), EKO degrades gracefully: (a) `search_and_ingest` propagates the
error as a structured `ResearchError::External`; (b) `enrich_from_europe_pmc`
collects per-sub-request warnings and persists partial data; (c) the
`AutoIngestResearchTool` wrapper never lets an ingestion failure break the
agent's search call. Two contract-honesty defects exist in the wrapper and
the enrichment timestamp path — see findings A-DOM-01-P2-01 and
A-DOM-01-P3-01.

## Expected Result

- A connector transport/HTTP/parse failure in `search_and_ingest` returns
  `Err(ResearchError::External(...))`, not a silent empty result.
- `enrich_from_europe_pmc` survives any combination of sub-request failures
  and surfaces them in `warnings`.
- The `AutoIngestResearchTool` wrapper does not propagate ingestion errors
  to the agent (the search tool still succeeds) — and **also** does not
  silently misrepresent that ingestion succeeded (defect: it does).
- `import_zotero` skips malformed items without aborting the whole import.

## Deviations

Two deviations from "graceful and honest" — both are filed findings, not
validation failures:

1. The `AutoIngestResearchTool` wrapper returns success without any signal
   that ingestion failed. The agent cannot distinguish "search succeeded
   and 25 results were persisted" from "search succeeded and 0 were
   persisted due to an I/O error". Filed as A-DOM-01-P2-01.
2. `enrich_from_europe_pmc` stamps `enriched_at = Some(now())` on a
   supplement that may be entirely empty. Filed as A-DOM-01-P3-01.

Neither deviation produces a panic, data corruption, or incorrect immediate
result; both are contract-honesty gaps where the API surface reports more
success than actually occurred.

## Conclusion

Supports findings: A-DOM-01-P2-01 (auto-ingest swallow), A-DOM-01-P3-01
(enriched_at timestamp honesty).

The validation confirms connectors fail through structured errors (not
panics, not silent empty results at the search level), and that the
EuropePMC enrichment degrades gracefully per sub-request. The
framework-level lack of `ToolFailure::Transient` (F-EXT-03-P3-02) means
transient HTTP blips on arxiv/pubmed/etc. fail the whole search — EKO
inherits this; it is not a new finding here.
