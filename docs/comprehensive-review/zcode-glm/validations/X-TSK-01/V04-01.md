  The `task_update` shape test confirms the EKO IPC DTO round-trips
  through `to_task_plan_patch` into the framework patch engine.

  → `RuntimeDagExecutor::execute`, dispatches `a` before `b`, and
  reaches `RunOutcome::Completed`.
