## Claim

package appears in `echo-agent-cli/Cargo.lock`; no Cargo.toml in
`echo-agent-cli/` declares a direct SQLite dependency; the
`echo-state`'s `sqlite` feature is not enabled transitively; and no
production source constructs a `SqliteStore`,
`SqliteConversationStore`, or `SqliteRuntimeStateStore`. Per AGENTS.md
"echo-agent-cli 不需要 SQLite" this invariant is mandatory.
