## Claim

`AnthropicClient::convert_request` faithfully maps every forwardable
neutral `ChatRequest` field onto the Anthropic `/v1/messages` wire body
(`AnthropicRequest`): `messages` are restructured into Anthropic's role +
content-block schema, system messages are extracted to the top-level
`system` field, the `temperature`/`max_tokens`/`tools`/`user_id` fields are
forwarded, `thinking` is translated by the per-protocol resolver,
`cache_hints` drives `cache_control` placement via `AnthropicCachePlan`,
and the documented drops (`tool_choice`, `response_format`, multi-`System`
collapse) are the only lossy mappings.

## Method

Static inspection of `convert_request`, the `AnthropicRequest` /
`AnthropicMessage` / `ContentBlock` / `AnthropicToolDef` wire types, the
`file_to_content_block` dispatch, and the production caller in `think.rs`.
