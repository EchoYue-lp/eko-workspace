## Claim

The Tauri command surface delegates to `AppState` services without duplicating
business authority. Specifically: (a) the command count is enumerable from
`generate_handler!`; (b) commands take `tauri::State<TauriState>` and route
through `state.app_state` rather than constructing their own services; (c) no
product-business logic is duplicated across surfaces or embedded in the
adapter/registration layer. If any of these fails, it becomes a finding.

## Method

Static enumeration and classification. Working directory:
`/Users/ls/MyWork/code/ylp_agent_learn/lp-agent`.

```text
1. Count and localize every command:
   grep -rc "#\[tauri::command\]" echo-agent-cli/src/tauri | sort

2. Confirm the registration is the single handler source:
   grep -n "invoke_handler\|generate_handler" echo-agent-cli/src/tauri/mod.rs

3. Confirm no command constructs AppState/AgentRuntime:
   grep -rn "AppState::from_shared\|AgentRuntime::bootstrap\|AppState::new" \
       echo-agent-cli/src/tauri

4. Classify a sample of commands as THIN vs FAT (deserialize → one call → DTO
   vs embeds product policy / orchestration). Read the body of:
   send_chat_message, save_conversation, set_permissions_mode, execute_sandbox,
   steer_chat_message, cancel_chat, enable_tool, list_conversations.

5. Cross-surface duplicate search for permission-mode canonicalization:
   grep -rn "auto-edit\|autoedit\|accept-edits\|full-auto\|fullauto\|bypass" \
       echo-agent-cli/src echo-agent-cli/echo-agent-app-core/src

6. Cross-surface duplicate search for tool-execution persistence:
   grep -rn "tool_executions\.start\|tool_executions\.finish\|tool_executions\.cancel" \
       echo-agent-cli/src/tauri
```

## Expected Result

- One `generate_handler!` listing all commands; the per-file `#[tauri::command]`
  count sums to the registered count.
- Zero command constructing `AppState` / `AgentRuntime`.
- Sampled commands are thin (per the `commands/mod.rs:1-7` docstring).
- No duplicated canonicalization or persistence across surfaces.

## Deviations

The validation's "no duplicated authority" claim is falsified in three places

deviations. The structural claims (single handler, no service construction)
hold.

## Conclusion

Supports the A-SRF-02 command-map findings. The Tauri command surface is
structurally sound (one handler, shared state, no service construction in
commands), but four commands and one registration-layer bridge embed product
business logic that should be in app-core (P3-01, P3-03, P2-03), and one
canonicalization is triplicated across surfaces with drift (P2-02). The
chat-turn lifecycle delegation to `drive_chat` is correct (no parallel
lifecycle). This is the sole evidence for P2-02, P2-03, P3-01, P3-03.
