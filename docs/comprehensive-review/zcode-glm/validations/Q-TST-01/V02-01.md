## Conclusion

The well-tested subsystems (CLI store/executor, subagent recovery, compression
invariants, post-M1 streaming channel) assert genuine production invariants
that would catch regressions. Two failure modes persist: (a) `react/tests.rs`
is dominated by grade-B builder/mock tests that certify no loop invariant
(supports Q-TST-01-P1-01), and (b) `test_sliding_window_compressor` is a
grade-C print-only fixture that gives false confidence (supports Q-TST-01-P3-01).
The Anthropic request-side test is grade-A for its scope; the absence of a
response-side fixture is the Q-TST-01-P1-02 gap.
