## Claim

Every non-GUI trigger routes through `drive_chat` (chat lane) or
`drive_agent_run` / `execute_run` (task lane). There is no parallel
chat driver and no parallel task-run pipeline. Concretely:

- REPL chat and channel chat call `drive_chat`.
- Cron fires call `launch_cron_run` → `launch_unattended_run` →
  `drive_unattended_run` → `drive_agent_run`.
- Background submissions call `execute_run` (when a plan exists) or
  `drive_unattended_run` (when not).
