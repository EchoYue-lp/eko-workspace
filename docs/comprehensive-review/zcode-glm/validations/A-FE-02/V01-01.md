## Claim

The three projection stores (subagent, tool-execution, task-runtime)
identify records by stable, backend-anchored keys that preserve
per-attempt identity. The subagent store encodes the attempt number
in the key itself; the task-runtime store uses a generation-protected
single-active-run model; the tool-execution store uses a dual
identity scheme (`tool.id` for live, `executionIdentity` for merge)
that is the structural root of the live-overwrite finding
(A-SRF-03-P2-01, confirmed).

## Expected Result

- `subagentRunStoreKey` composes `${runId}\u0000${subagentRunId}`
  where `subagentRunId` is the per-attempt execution id. Two retries
  (attempt 1, attempt 2) produce TWO different keys. The key is
  stable for the lifetime of one attempt.
- `executionIdentity` composes `${ownerKey}\u0000${call_id}` and is
  used ONLY by the merge/hydrate path. The live `ingest` path keys
  by `tool.id` instead — a different identity. This duality is
  intentional (live tools carry a backend-assigned id; merge dedupes
  by semantic identity) but creates the live-overwrite window.
- `taskRuntimeStore` uses module-level generation counters
  (`loadGeneration`, `refreshRequestGeneration`) that are
  monotonically bumped on every load/refresh start; a `set` checks
  its captured generation against the current one before applying.
  `lastSeq` (string) is the incremental-polling cursor, reset to
  `'0'` on conversation switch.

## Deviations

One finding (cross-referenced, not a deviation from the claim):

The tool-execution store's dual identity means the live `ingest`
path (keyed by `tool.id`) and the merge/hydrate path (keyed by
`executionIdentity`) can disagree on whether two payloads represent
the same execution. When a live `started` and a hydrated terminal
share the same `executionIdentity` but differ in `tool.id` (e.g.
`runtime-tool:...` vs a backend-assigned `detail-1`), the live
overwrite lands on a different store slot than the hydrate merge,
and the two terminals coexist until the next hydrate reconciles
them. This is the mechanism behind A-SRF-03-P2-01 and is documented
in A-FE-02-P3-03 (test gap). Not a deviation from the V01 claim —
the identity models ARE stable — but the duality is the load-bearing
context for V02.
