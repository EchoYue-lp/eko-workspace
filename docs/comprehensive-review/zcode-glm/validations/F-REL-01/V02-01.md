- `CircuitBreaker`: failures increment a counter; on reaching the threshold the
  state moves Closed -> Open; after a cooldown `try_advance` moves Open ->
  HalfOpen; a successful probe in HalfOpen moves back to Closed, a failure back
  to Open.
- `RetryPolicy::delay_for`: delay grows exponentially with attempt number,
  capped by `max_delay`, with optional jitter; `no_retry` yields zero retries.
- `TokenBudget::allocate`: the four allocations sum within `total_window` and
  expose a remaining/usage view via `report`.
